## 🔥 Top Story: Anthropic vs. The Pentagon — AI Ethics Crisis Escalates

The biggest AI story of the week is the dramatic escalation of the standoff between **Anthropic** and the **U.S. Department of Defense**.

### What Happened
- Anthropic has **refused to allow the Pentagon to use Claude for domestic mass surveillance or autonomous weapons systems**. Negotiations collapsed over a clause barring the Pentagon from using Claude to analyze bulk commercial data.
- **Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth** gave Anthropic a Friday deadline to grant the military full access to its AI models, or risk losing up to **$200 million in contracts**.
- A top Pentagon official (Under Secretary Emil Michael) revealed on the *All-In* podcast that the military's reliance on Anthropic's Claude came as a "shocking realization" — Claude is already playing a key role in the **U.S. military campaign in Iran**.
- **President Trump** announced a 6-month phase-out period for agencies using Anthropic products. **Treasury and HHS** have already terminated all Anthropic use, directing employees to use ChatGPT and Gemini instead.
- Anthropic CEO **Dario Amodei** called OpenAI's messaging around its own military deal **"straight up lies"**, saying Altman falsely presents himself as a "peacemaker."
- Anthropic has said it will **challenge the Pentagon in court** once it receives formal notice of penalties.

### Industry Fallout
- **~900 tech workers** (nearly 100 from OpenAI, ~800 from Google) signed an open letter titled *"We Will Not Be Divided"* urging Google and OpenAI to adopt Anthropic's red lines on military AI.
- Google employees are calling for **military limits on AI** amid Iran strikes and the Anthropic fallout.
- Cloud vendors (Google, Microsoft) are reassuring customers that Anthropic's tools remain available outside defense projects.
- The *New York Times* published an op-ed titled **"The Future We Feared Is Already Here"**, calling the dispute a window into how unprepared society is for AI governance.
